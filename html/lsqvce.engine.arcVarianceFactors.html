lsqvce.engine.arcVarianceFactors

Relative per-arc variance factors within a group.

Description

out = LSQVCE.ENGINE.ARCVARIANCEFACTORS(Q, T, sigma2)
out = LSQVCE.ENGINE.ARCVARIANCEFACTORS(Q, T, sigma2, 'Name', value, ...)

Estimates Ellmer's (2018) arc-wise variance factors: the RELATIVE weight of
each short arc with respect to the other arcs of the SAME observation type
(eqs. 6.5.27-6.5.30). Where the PSD amplitudes are found by estimating one
frequency across all arcs, the arc factors are the mirror image -- the
CUMULATIVE amplitude over all frequencies for one arc:

    alpha2_m = sum_i Q(i, m) / sum_i T(i, m)                      (6.5.27)
    gamma2_m = alpha2_m * sigma2_m                                (6.5.28)
    gammaBar = mean over arcs of gamma_m                          (6.5.29)
    sigma2hat_m = gamma2_m / gammaBar^2                           (6.5.30)

WHY THE gamma DETOUR (eq. 6.5.28 discussion) -- do not "simplify" this

The estimate is NOT simply sigma2hat_m = alpha2_m * sigma2_m. The overall
magnitude of the covariance matrix has ALREADY been adjusted by the
per-frequency PSD amplitudes (LSQVCE.ENGINE.PSDVCE), so taking alpha2_m * sigma2_m
directly would count the same variance component TWICE. The intermediate
gamma2_m is therefore normalised by the mean over arcs, leaving the arc
factors carrying only the RELATIVE arc-to-arc weighting while the
covariance function carries the absolute magnitude and structure.

The Mean: Robust By Default

'robust' (default, what GROOPS does, eq. 6.5.57): the mean of the CENTRAL
  HALF of the sorted gamma_m,  gammaBar = (2/M) * sum_{m=M/4}^{3M/4} gamma_m,
  ignoring outliers at either tail. Recommended: a handful of bad arcs
  (attitude-hold periods, manoeuvres) otherwise drag the normalisation.
'plain' (eq. 6.5.29): the arithmetic mean of all gamma_m. This is the only
  variant for which mean(gamma_m/gammaBar) == 1 EXACTLY, i.e. the mean of
  the arc factors sqrt(sigma2hat_m) is exactly 1. The robust variant gives
  up that exact property in exchange for outlier resistance -- with planted
  outliers the plain mean was 1.69 against a robust 1.24 and a median 1.24.

Input

  Q        K x M double     per-arc numerators, one COLUMN per arc; the
                            joint estimator passes ONE row, the arc's whole
                            law T as the component: Q = etilde' T etilde,
                            T = trace(Rbar T) (LSQVCE.KERNEL.REDUNDANCYLAGS
                            .omegaLaw / .sLaw, WP-132; r' Omega / r' s
                            before, which assumed T Toeplitz) -- the exact
                            Helmert factor of the arc.
  T        K x M double     the matching denominators -- a redundancy,
                            which a probe estimate can make <= 0 on a short
                            arc with local parameters: such an arc is HELD
                            (alpha2 = 1, .held; WP-134)
  sigma2   M x 1 double     the CURRENT arc variance factors sigma2_m that
                            were used in the weighting (eq. 6.5.28 needs them).

OPTIONS (Name/Value)
  'MeanType'  char    'robust' (default) | 'plain'   (see above)
  'TrimFrac'  1x1     fraction trimmed from EACH tail for 'robust'
                      (default 0.25, i.e. Ellmer's central half)
  'ClampEps'  1x1     floor for the NORMALISED arc factors (> 0, default
                      1e-8; sigma2hat is already divided by gammaBar^2, so
                      healthy factors are O(1)): any sigma2hat_m below it --
                      negative gamma2 from a negative alpha2, or a
                      degenerate ratio -- is clamped to ClampEps, warned
                      about with the arc indices (id
                      lsqvce:arcVarianceFactors:clamped) and flagged in
                      .clamped. A negative variance factor must never leave
                      this function: it weights a covariance downstream.

Output

out   1x1 struct
  .sigma2   M x 1   the updated arc variance factors sigma2hat_m
  .alpha2   M x 1   the raw cumulative factors alpha2_m (eq. 6.5.27)
  .gamma2   M x 1   the intermediate gamma2_m (eq. 6.5.28)
  .gammaBar 1x1     the normalising mean actually used
  .clamped  M x 1 logical  true where sigma2hat_m fell below ClampEps
                    (a degenerate alpha2) and was clamped to it (WP-35,
                    fixes F-05); all-false on healthy input
  .held     M x 1 logical  true where the denominator was not positive
                    (no redundancy to estimate from): alpha2 = 1, the arc
                    keeps sigma2_m (WP-134; before, a negative probe
                    redundancy gave alpha2 < 0, the clamp floored the
                    VARIANCE at 1e-8 and the arc got 1e8 times the weight)
  NOTE  the joint estimator reads the ABSOLUTE factors gamma2_m (R7-09,
        WP-116: .sigma2 * gammaBar^2) -- its law is a shape, so the level
        lives in the factors; the relative form above is Ellmer's
  .meanType char    which mean was applied
  .pooled   1x1     the POOLED Helmert factor of the group as ONE
                    component, sum_m(alpha2_m-numerator * sigma2_m) /
                    sum_m(denominator) = sum_m q_m sigma2_m / sum_m t_m
                    over the arcs with a finite, positive denominator
                    (NaN when there is none). It is the level of a
                    stream without arc factors (LSQVCE.ENGINE.ADJUSTVCEJOINT,
                    WP-125/126): long arcs weigh by their redundancy,
                    where mean(gamma2) weighs every arc alike

Example

a = lsqvce.engine.arcVarianceFactors(qa, ta, sig2);   % qa = r*Omega, ta = r*s
sig2 = a.sigma2;                 % feed back into the next iteration
mean(sqrt(a.sigma2))             % == 1 exactly only for 'plain'

Accuracy

Exact algebra; the statistics live in Q and T (T may be a probe
estimate). .sigma2 equals the closed form of eqs. 6.5.28-6.5.30 (HANDOVER
6.1.4 row "Ellmer (2018) doctoral thesis") to 4 eps relative, CI-measured
2.6e-16 (test_ellmer (test_arcVarianceFactors_noop_on_healthy)); a
per-arc scale of Q and T moves alpha2 by under 1e-12 relative (test_ellmer
(test_arcVarianceFactors_alpha2_is_blind_to_the_arcs_own_factor)); .pooled
is 2060/2030 to 4 eps (test_ellmer
(test_arcVarianceFactors_pooled_level_weighs_by_redundancy)). WP-139.

Errors

  lsqvce:arcVarianceFactors:arcMismatch  the arc list disagrees with the
                                accumulated arcs
