lsqvce.kernel.whitenessCorr

Largest absolute autocorrelation over a lag range.

Syntax

maxCorr = lsqvce.kernel.whitenessCorr(series, maxLag)

Description

A cheap whiteness statistic. The series is mean-removed and normalised by its lag-0 energy, so 0 <= maxCorr <= 1. For white noise the sample autocorrelations sit inside roughly +/- k/sqrt(n); compare maxCorr against a band such as whiteFactor/sqrt(numel(series)) to decide whiteness.

Input

series   nx1 | 1xn   double    Time series (a residual stream). Required.
                                Fewer than 2 elements returns 0.
maxLag   1x1         integer   Highest lag to test, >= 1. Required.
                                Internally capped at numel(series)-1.

Output

maxCorr  1x1         double    max over lags 1..maxLag of
                                |sum(x_t x_{t+lag})| / sum(x_t^2), in [0,1].

Example

w = randn(4000,1);
assert(lsqvce.kernel.whitenessCorr(w, 5) < 3/sqrt(4000));   % white -> below band
