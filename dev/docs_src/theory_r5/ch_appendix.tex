\begin{principle}[As of 5.0 --- a dated record]
This table is version 5.0 as shipped on 3 September 2026. The option set has moved since (5.1 \ldots{} 6.1: \code{MinPasses} and \code{LogFile} retired, \code{Robust} composable with \code{Estimate="noise"}, 17 knobs); the current table is the Manual, chapter 4 (R6 WP-106).
\end{principle}

version~5.0 ships (\code{RELEASE\_NOTES.md} and \code{dev/HANDOVER.md}

Deviations from the plan, and why: \code{noiseLaw} was not created (the PSD
spec, covModel's resampling and two policy maps are the mechanism; a class
would add a name); \code{iterate}/\code{policy}/\code{gate}/\code{passRecord}
are the joint driver's loop, fit's policy mapping, \code{stopReason} and
\code{history}; \code{Noise.Sigma0Prior} was not needed (the whitened frame's
a-priori $\sigma_0$ is~1 by construction); \code{Parallel.Axis} waits for its
measurement; the per-pass plot hook was first retired and then restored as
\code{OnPass} because the project's opening brief required it -- the rule
recorded in HANDOVER \S12.9 (5c$'$): a review-plan item never outranks an
opening-brief requirement.
